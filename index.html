03 Process

Four
movements

Two to six weeks, concept to master. Fixed fee, no hourly.

  1. 01 — Interrogate

    Find the one image

    Before a single frame is generated we agree on the single image the whole film is built to earn. Everything after that is service to it.

  2. 02 — Build the world

    Lock the visual DNA

    Character sheets, palette, stock, lens set, lighting logic. Written down and frozen, so shot forty still looks like shot one.

  3. 03 — Shoot

    Direct the machine

    Structured prompts per shot, generated in passes, selected the way a director selects takes — for performance, not for novelty.

  4. 04 — Finish

    Cut, grade, deliver

    Edit to picture, sound design, film emulation, and masters in every ratio your channels actually need.

04 About

SV / 2026

I spent twelve years making other people's footage look expensive. Now I make the footage too.

My background is post — colour and finishing for agencies who cared about a quarter-stop. That work taught me the only thing that transfers cleanly into generative film: an image is convincing because of its logic, not its resolution. Where the light comes from. What the lens would actually do at that distance. Why the character is standing there at all.

Most AI video fails on exactly that. It is sharp and weightless. My job is to put the weight back — to direct these tools with the same discipline a crew brings to a set, so the result reads as a film someone made, not as output.

I work with a small number of clients at a time, usually brands, labels and directors who need something built that cannot be shot. If that's the problem in front of you, write to me.

  • Based Remote — worldwide
  • Clients Brands, labels, agencies, festivals
  • Tools Seedance · Veo · Nuke · Resolve · Houdini
  • Terms Fixed fee, 50% up front

He is the only person we've worked with who treats a generated shot like a take that can be wrong. That single instinct is the whole difference.

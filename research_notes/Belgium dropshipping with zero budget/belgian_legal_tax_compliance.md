## 1. Business setup: KBO registration, self-employed status, social contributions, legal form, side-income regimes

### Takeaway
A sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak / entreprise individuelle) registered in the KBO/BCE is enough. No company (BV/SRL) is needed, and since October 2025 no region requires a business-management certificate. One-off registration costs €111.50 in 2026. The main recurring cost is social contributions, and this depends heavily on status. A full-time self-employed person pays at least €890.42 per quarter (€459.82 per quarter as a first-time "primostarter"). A side activity (bijberoep) pays €0 below €1,922.16 of annual taxable income and €98.51 per quarter at the minimum. A student-entrepreneur pays €0 below €8,687.04. Online retail does not fall under the tax-favoured "deeleconomie" / P2P side-income regime, which covers services only.

### Cited Findings
**KBO/BCE registration (ondernemingsloket / guichet d'entreprises)**
- In 2026, registration at an ondernemingsloket costs €111.50 (VAT-exempt). Every later change, such as an extra establishment unit, an address change or a deregistration, costs the same amount — [VLAIO: Een ondernemingsnummer aanvragen](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/opstart-van-je-onderneming/een-ondernemingsnummer-aanvragen); [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/starten-als-zelfstandige/een-eigen-zaak-starten/ondernemingsnummer-aanvragen)
- Securex charges €111.50 per establishment unit or address. Activating the VAT number through Securex's loket is a separate €60 (excl. VAT) — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/starten-als-zelfstandige/een-eigen-zaak-starten/ondernemingsnummer-aanvragen)
- Bobex quotes a loose range of €105–110 excl. VAT as a guide price. This is an outlier, and the VLAIO/Securex figure of €111.50 is the better-supported one — [Bobex](https://www.bobex.be/nl-be/sociaal-secretariaat/vennootschap-oprichten/welk-ondernemingsloket-kiezen/)
- The 2024 KBO fee was €105.50, which shows the fee is indexed every year — [Accountable: btw-nummer aanvragen](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/btw-nummer-aanvragen/)
- The VAT number is "BE" plus the enterprise number and must be activated with form 604A. Since 12 July 2021 the form must be filed online via MyMinfin (application e604). You can file it yourself, or through a proxy or a recognised ondernemingsloket, and the loket charges a fee — [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/btw-nummer-aanvragen/); [Forum for the Future: e604 via MyMinfin](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/aanvang-wijziging-en-einde-activiteit-de-toepassing-e604-is-nu-ook-rechtstreeks-toegankelijk-via-myminfin/25946); [Vlaanderen form](https://lv.vlaanderen.be/media/8238/download)

**Basic business-management knowledge (basiskennis bedrijfsbeheer)**
- The requirement has been abolished in all three regions. Flanders dropped it in 2018, Brussels in January 2024, and Wallonia on 1 October 2025 (decree approved 24 Sept 2025, published 3 Oct 2025). Professional-competence rules still apply to some regulated trades (e.g. bakers, roofers, restaurateurs), mainly in Brussels and Wallonia — [Securex (Wallonia)](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/nieuws/basiskennis-bedrijfsbeheer-verdwijnt-als-startvoorwaarde-in-waalse-gewest); [Partena (Wallonia/Brussels)](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/onze-inzichten/infoflashes/wallonie-schaft-de-verplichting-tot-basiskennis-bedrijfsbeheer-voor); [Accountable (Flanders 2018)](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/diploma-bedrijfsbeheer-voor-zelfstandigen/); [Xerius](https://www.xerius.be/nl-be/zelfstandig-worden/bedrijfsbeheer-en-beroepskennis)

**Social insurance fund (sociaal verzekeringsfonds / caisse d'assurances sociales)**
- You must join a social insurance fund before the activity starts — [BILLY: sociaal verzekeringsfonds kiezen](https://billy.tech/nl/gids/onderneming-starten/stappen/administratieve-formaliteiten/sociaal-verzekeringsfonds-kiezen/)
- Funds add management fees of 3.05% to 4.25% of contributions, and each fund sets its own rate. The source is undated, but a 2021 RSVZ committee advice gives the same band — [BILLY](https://billy.tech/nl/gids/onderneming-starten/stappen/administratieve-formaliteiten/sociaal-verzekeringsfonds-kiezen/); [RSVZ Algemeen Beheerscomité advies 2021/19](https://www.rsvz-inasti.fgov.be/sites/rsvz/files/publication/algemeen_beheerscomite_advies_202119.pdf)

**2026 social contributions (RSVZ/INASTI table 2026)**
- **Main occupation (hoofdberoep):** the rate is 20.50% of income up to €75,024.54. The minimum base is €17,374.08, which gives a minimum of **€890.42 per quarter** in 2026, excluding management fees — [RSVZ bijdragennota 2026 tabel](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/sites/default/files/content/docs/nl/sociaal-beleid-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/bijdragennota_2026_tabel_nl.pdf); [Accountable: sociale bijdragen](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/sociale-bijdragen)
- **Primostarters** (first-time self-employed in a main occupation) have a lower minimum base of €8,972.07, giving **€459.82 per quarter** — [RSVZ 2026 table](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/sites/default/files/content/docs/nl/sociaal-beleid-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/bijdragennota_2026_tabel_nl.pdf). Partena's 2026 schedule shows €479.13 — [Partena barema 2026](https://www.partena-professional.be/sites/default/files/uploads/Administratieve%20docs%20%26%20forms/ASI/NL-Bareme%202026.pdf). The VLAIO page says €341.30 for the first quarter and €459.82 for the next three, but it cites reference-year 2023 elsewhere, so it may be outdated — [VLAIO Starterskorting](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/verlaging-sociale-bijdragen-voor-primostarters-starterskorting). The 2025 figure was €478.67 — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/rechten-plichten/hoofdberoep/plichten/sociale-bijdragen-betalen/hoe-worden-de-sociale-bijdragen-berekend/verlaagde-minimumbijdragen-(primostarters))
- **Side occupation (bijberoep):** the minimum is **€98.51 per quarter**. A side-occupation earner with 2026 taxable income below **€1,922.16** pays no social contributions. Above that, 20.5% applies — [Accountable: sociale bijdragen](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/sociale-bijdragen). Partena's 2026 schedule gives €102.65 per quarter in provisional contributions for starting side-occupation earners — [Partena barema 2026](https://www.partena-professional.be/sites/default/files/uploads/Administratieve%20docs%20%26%20forms/ASI/NL-Bareme%202026.pdf)
- In 2026, regular provisional contributions are calculated on 2023 income (the three-year lag) — [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/sociale-bijdragen)
- **Conditions for bijberoep status:** you must be an employee working at least 50% (at least 235 hours per quarter). Civil servants need 50% employment and at least 8 months or 200 days. Teaching has different thresholds: 60% for statutory staff and 50% for non-tenured staff — [VLAIO: voorwaarden bijberoep](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/ondernemen-bijberoep/voorwaarden-om-te-starten-bijberoep); [Vlaanderen.be](https://www.vlaanderen.be/starten-met-een-onderneming/zelfstandige-in-bijberoep)
- Someone with no replacement income or qualifying employment is treated as self-employed in a main occupation — [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/werkloosheid-en-ondernemen/je-hebt-een-zelfstandig-bijberoep-en-wordt)

**Student-entrepreneur (student-zelfstandige)**
- Contributions are exempt if net taxable annual income stays below **€8,687.04**, but the exemption is not automatic and must be requested. If in doubt, VLAIO advises paying the €98.51 minimum. Between €8,687.04 and €17,374.08, 20.5% applies only to the part above €8,687.04; on €10,000, for example, the contribution is €269.16 for the year. Above €17,374.08, main-occupation rules apply — [VLAIO: student-zelfstandige FAQ](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/student-zelfstandige/veelgestelde-vragen/veelgestelde-vragen-na-de-opstart-van-je-student-onderneming)
- Traditional conditions are age 18–25 and enrolment for at least 27 credits per year (or 17 lesson hours per week) with regular attendance — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/inspiratie/ondernemerschap/starten/een-voordelig-dubbelleven-als-student-zelfstandige-5-weetjes); [RSVZ advies 2016/14](https://www.rsvz-inasti.fgov.be/sites/rsvz/files/publication/algemeen_beheerscomite_advies_201614.pdf)
- A reform (Partena, updated 6 July 2026) lets the status continue after age 25 under conditions, for example if you already held it in Q3 of the year you turn 25 and keep studying. It also adds exceptions to the 27-credit rule for compulsory internships and theses — [Partena: Hervorming statuut student-zelfstandige](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/node/22542); [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/wijzigingen-betreffende-het-statuut-van-student-zelfstandige/30874)
- Student-entrepreneur income can also affect the family allowance (Groeipakket) — [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/student-zelfstandige/statuut-van-student-zelfstandige)

**Legal form: sole proprietorship vs BV/SRL**
- A Belgian BV/SRL has no minimum capital, but founders must show "sufficient starting capital" in a mandatory financial plan, and a notarial deed is required — [Bobex: vennootschap oprichten](https://www.bobex.be/nl-be/sociaal-secretariaat/vennootschap-oprichten/); [Partena: BV oprichten](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/starten-als-zelfstandige/statuut-en-rechtsvorm/besloten-vennootschap-bv-oprichten)

**"Onbelaste bijverdiensten" / deeleconomie (P2P platform) regime**
- The regime covers **services** a private individual provides to another private individual through a **recognised electronic platform** (or a government-organised one). Further conditions are that the services go only to natural persons acting outside a professional activity, that income is limited to €7,460 per year (indexed gross, per a 2025 Chamber document), and that the platform handles payment. Renting goods (e.g. Airbnb) and providing capital are explicitly excluded. The Brussels labour court held that meal couriers cannot use the regime because the meals were not made by private individuals — [Kamer doc 56K0514](https://www.dekamer.be/FLWB/Pdf/56/0514/56K0514005.pdf); [Unizo: deeleconomie](https://www.unizo.be/advies/actief-binnen-de-deeleconomie-hoeveel-belastingen-betalen-wat-met-sociale-bijdragen)
- The tax rate is 20% after a 50% flat cost deduction (effective 10%), and the income is exempt from social contributions. Platforms issue fiche 281.29. Above the ceiling, everything is taxed as ordinary professional income — [Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/advies/actief-binnen-de-deeleconomie-hoeveel-belastingen-betalen-wat-met-sociale-bijdragen); [VAEE PDF](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/14a7bbf4-2fc6-47c7-a851-bad3a83f2ad3/-/inline/no/PAR_VAEE_Inkomsten-uit-de-deeleconomie.pdf)
- The ceiling is disputed. A 2025 Chamber document gives €7,460 and an older ETUC report €6,540. A commercial blog (March 2026) claims €7,700 for 2026, but it is not authoritative. A December 2025 Chamber resolution proposal targets abuse of the regime by platforms — [Kamer doc 56K0514](https://www.dekamer.be/FLWB/Pdf/56/0514/56K0514001.pdf); [iambeezy blog](https://blog.iambeezy.app/fr/economie-collaborative-belgique-2026-plafond-fiscal-7700-euros/)
- Test-Aankoop warns that side income above the limit is professional income, taxed at your highest marginal rate (up to 50%) — [Test-Aankoop: Klus je bij?](https://www.test-aankoop.be/geld/belastingen/nieuws/extra-inkomen)

### Inferences
- Online retail of goods bought for resale, including dropshipping, **is not covered** by the deeleconomie/P2P regime. That regime is limited to services between private persons via recognised platforms. Selling goods with a profit motive is a commercial activity that requires KBO registration and self-employed status.
- The Partena figures of €102.65 (bijberoep) and €479.13 (primostarter) equal the RSVZ figures of €98.51 and €459.82 multiplied by about 1.042. They are therefore the RSVZ contributions plus about 4.2% fund management fees, not contradictory figures.
- The cheapest legal route for someone with €0 is: (a) student-entrepreneur, if eligible, with an exemption request and income below €8,687.04 (€0 contributions); (b) bijberoep alongside a job of at least 50%, with profit below €1,922.16 (€0 final contributions, though provisional contributions may be billed in the first years); (c) main occupation as a primostarter at about €1,839/year plus fees. A non-student without a qualifying job cannot use bijberoep and is treated as a main-occupation self-employed person.
- A BV/SRL is unnecessary and costly at this scale (notary, financial plan, annual accounts filing). An eenmanszaak suffices, but the owner has unlimited personal liability, which matters given the product-liability exposure (see section 3).

### Gaps
- The full RSVZ 2026 PDF could not be opened. The €1,922.16 bijberoep exemption threshold and the €98.51 minimum come from Accountable and VLAIO, not the RSVZ document itself.
- No 2026 Belgian notary fee figure for a BV/SRL was found. Dutch-source figures (€1,200–3,000) were excluded as non-Belgian.
- The official 2026 indexed deeleconomie ceiling was not confirmed (€7,460 vs €7,700).
- It was not confirmed whether funds bill provisional bijberoep contributions by default to starters who expect less than €1,922.16, or only after regularisation.

## 2. VAT and customs: franchise scheme, OSS/IOSS, €22/€150 thresholds, €3 duty, €2 handling fee, deemed supplier, e-invoicing

### Takeaway
A Belgian small business can use the VAT franchise (vrijstellingsregeling / franchise) up to **€25,000** annual turnover. From 1 January 2025 it sits alongside an EU-wide €100,000 cross-border SME limit. Under the franchise you charge no VAT, but **import VAT on goods from China becomes a non-deductible cost**. Intra-EU B2C distance sales above **€10,000** (EU-wide) are taxed in the customer's country, via OSS. For non-EU goods, the €22 import-VAT exemption ended on 1 July 2021. Since **1 July 2026**, a temporary **€3 customs duty per tariff category** applies to consignments of €150 or less (until 1 July 2028). An EU **€2 per-item "Union handling fee"** was set by delegated act on 21 Sept 2026 and is expected from **1 November 2026**, but as of 24 Oct 2026 it was not yet confirmed in force. B2B e-invoicing via Peppol has been mandatory in Belgium since 1 January 2026, and franchise businesses must at least be able to receive e-invoices.

### Cited Findings
**Belgian VAT franchise (art. 56bis W.BTW)**
- The 2026 threshold is €25,000 excl. VAT annual turnover. VAT units and certain sectors (works on immovable property, hospitality, trade in old materials) are excluded. Starters get a pro-rata threshold; for example, a start on 1 July gives about €12,603 — [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/wat-houdt-de-btw-vrijstellingsregeling-voor-kleine-ondernemingen-in/30644); [Securex lex4you](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/nieuws/btw-vrijstellingsregeling-voor-kleine-ondernemingen-wat-is-dat)
- If you exceed the threshold by at most 10% (up to €27,500), you keep the exemption until year-end and then move to the normal regime. A larger overshoot means switching immediately. After leaving the scheme, you can return only from 1 January of the second year after — [Accountable: wijzigingen 2025](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/wijzigingen-2025-btw-vrijstelling-kleine-ondernemingen/); [Accountable help centre](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/help-center/alles-wat-je-moet-weten-over-de-status-van-btw-vrijstelling-voor-kleine-ondernemingen/)
- **2025 reform:** from 1 January 2025, implementing Directive 2020/285, there is a dual threshold. The national €25,000 stays unchanged, and an EU-wide total turnover limit of €100,000 lets small businesses use the exemption in other member states without registering or filing there — [VLAIO: Nieuwe regeling btw-vrijstelling](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/nieuws/nieuwe-regeling-btw-vrijstelling-kleine-ondernemingen); [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/wijzigingen-2025-btw-vrijstelling-kleine-ondernemingen/)
- A Chamber document notes the €25,000 ceiling has not been raised since 2016 and has eroded in real terms. No change had been confirmed — [Kamer doc 56K1205](https://www.dekamer.be/FLWB/PDF/56/1205/56K1205005.pdf)
- An exempt small business remains a VAT taxable person and needs a valid VAT number. It cannot deduct VAT on purchases, and import VAT it pays is a definitive cost — [ClearTax BE](https://www.cleartax.com/be/nl/vat-exemption-scheme-belgium); [Loyens & Loeff e-commerce brochure](https://www.loyensloeff.com/nl/l-l_e-commerce-nl.pdf); [juridischforum (forum, low reliability)](https://juridischforum.be/viewtopic.php?p=632552)
- An exempt small business falls in the "group of four" not subject to intra-EU acquisition rules (art. 56bis), and the €10,000 distance-selling tolerance can be combined with the small-business scheme — [practicali](https://www.practicali.be/blog/intracommunautaire-afstandsverkopen-en-de-oss-aangifte)

**Intra-EU distance sales and OSS**
- Since 1 July 2021, national distance-selling thresholds (Belgium's was €35,000) have been replaced by one **€10,000 EU-wide threshold**, which also covers B2C electronic services. Below it, Belgian VAT (or the Belgian franchise) applies. Above it, the VAT of the customer's country applies for the rest of that year and the next. OSS is an optional simplification that lets you declare foreign VAT through one Belgian return — [practicali](https://www.practicali.be/blog/intracommunautaire-afstandsverkopen-en-de-oss-aangifte); [SBB](https://www.sbb.be/nl/node/2123); [Accountable FR: le régime OSS](https://www.accountable.eu/fr-be/blog/le-regime-oss/)
- You can waive the threshold and always charge destination-country VAT, a choice binding for at least 2 years — [Accountable FR](https://www.accountable.eu/fr-be/blog/le-regime-oss/)

**Imports from China: €22 exemption, IOSS**
- On 1 July 2021, the import-VAT exemption for small consignments up to €22 was abolished. IOSS was introduced for consignments with an intrinsic value of **€150 or less**: the seller charges destination VAT at checkout and declares it via a monthly IOSS return, and import is then VAT-exempt — [SBB](https://www.sbb.be/nl/node/2123); [practicali](https://www.practicali.be/blog/webverkopen-nieuwe-regels-inzake-btw-op-1-juli-2021)
- According to Commission guidance (as reported), an EU-established seller does not need an IOSS intermediary, while non-EU sellers generally must appoint one. The IOSS number is not a VAT number — [Kintsugi: What is IOSS](https://trykintsugi.com/blog/what-is-ioss-import-one-stop-shop.md); [Stripe](https://stripe.com/resources/more/ioss-and-eu-vat); [Metapack](https://help.metapack.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4402342103697)
- Without IOSS, the consumer or carrier pays import VAT at Belgian rates on customs value plus import duties — [VRT NWS: pakjestaks FAQ, 30 June 2026](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/06/30/pakjestaks-3-euro-meest-gestelde-vragen/)

**Marketplace deemed-supplier rule**
- Under Art. 14a of the VAT Directive, a platform that facilitates distance sales of imported goods in consignments of €150 or less is treated as having bought and resold them, so it collects the VAT. This applies regardless of where the underlying seller is established — [Irish Revenue: deemed supplier](https://www.revenue.ie/en/vat/vat-ecommerce/deemed-supplier/index.aspx); [NWB Art. 14a text](https://datenbank.nwb.de/Dokument/452295_14a)
- Directive (EU) 2025/516 reportedly replaces Art. 14a and drops the €150 limit, so deemed-supplier status would cover all facilitated imports. One source dates this from 1 January 2027, but the date was not verified — [EUR-Lex C/2024/1579](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2024/1579/oj/eng); [Council doc ST-12336-2025](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-12336-2025-REV-1/en/pdf); [EESC opinion](https://www.eesc.europa.eu/en/our-work/opinions-information-reports/opinions/vat-rules-relating-taxable-persons-who-facilitate-distance-sales-imported-goods)

**End of the €150 customs-duty exemption: the temporary €3 duty (in force)**
- ECOFIN agreed on 12 December 2025 and the Council gave final adoption on **11 February 2026**. A flat customs duty of **€3** applies to each **item category** in small parcels valued under €150 sent to EU consumers, from **1 July 2026 to 1 July 2028**, when the customs data hub takes over and normal tariffs apply. Example: a parcel with two toys, one coat and three bottles of shampoo pays €9 (three categories) — [Council press release 12 Dec 2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/); [Council press release 11 Feb 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/11/council-gives-final-green-light-to-new-customs-duty-rules-for-small-parcels/); [Council policy page](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-action-influx-of-small-parcels/)
- The legal act is Council Regulation (EU) 2026/382. The duty applies per distinct tariff classification in a consignment, not per parcel or per unit, and to B2C distance sales **regardless of VAT regime** (IOSS, special arrangements or standard import VAT) and declaration type (H1, H6, H7). Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1200 adjusts the mechanics — [EC Access2Markets](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/eu-applies-eu3-customs-duty-item-low-value-e-commerce-consignments); [Ship24](https://www.ship24.com/blog/the-eus-euro3-low-value-import-duty-what-changed-on-1-july-2026); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ie/en/insights/news-opinions/eu-3-duty-on-low-value-imports); [EC guidance, 8 June 2026](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-and-legal-text-temporary-flat-fee-low-value-imports-which-will-apply-until-1-july-2028-2026-06-08_en)
- The Council framed the measure as covering goods from IOSS-registered non-EU sellers (about 93% of e-commerce flows into the EU) — [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/). The Commission's guidance is broader (all regimes, as above). Parcels shipped from inside the EU are not subject to it — [Test-Aankoop](https://www.test-aankoop.be/familie-prive/consumentenrechten/nieuws/belasting-kleine-pakketten-aankoop-buiten-de-eu)
- Belgium dropped its planned national €2 parcel tax (announced November 2025) in favour of the EU €3 duty — [VRT NWS EN, 26 Dec 2025](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2025/12/26/belgium-ditches-2-parcel-tax-in-favour-of-3-european-parcel-tax/); [VRT NWS, 24 Nov 2025](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/11/24/pakjes-2-euro-webshops-buiten-europa-taks-belasting-regering-she/)

**EU "Union handling fee" (pending, expected 1 November 2026)**
- The Commission adopted a delegated regulation, C(2026) 6694 of **21 September 2026**, setting the Union handling fee at **€2 per item** on goods sold at a distance from non-EU countries, **at any value** (not just €150 or less). It is non-refundable even if goods are returned and stacks on top of the €3 duty. It applies once published in the OJ and unless Parliament or the Council objects, with **1 November 2026** as the expected date. Member states must stop applying national handling fees once it applies — [VitalLaw/Wolters Kluwer](https://www.vitallaw.com/news/eu-confirms-new-e-commerce-handling-fee-will-be-eur2-per-item/gdn01174716); [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/18538/eu-handling-fee-2-euro-non-eu-parcels); [eCommerce Accountants](https://www.ecommerceaccountants.co.uk/post/eu-handling-fee-parcels-november-2026); [Dimweigh](https://dimweigh.com/blog/eu-2-euro-handling-fee-november-2026); [SGS e-Customs, 14 Oct 2026](https://ecustoms.sgs.com/2026/10/14/eu-takes-action-to-curb-influx-of-small-parcels-without-customs-duties/)
- **Conflict on the counting unit:** most sources say "per item". Royal Mail describes it as "€2 per line", and the Dutch customs position (as reported) is per declaration line. An "item" can group pieces that share specific data — [Royal Mail European Trade Insights](https://www.royalmail.com/europeantradeinsights); [Statrys](https://statrys.com/blog/eu-3-euro-customs-duty-small-parcels); [Holdwise](https://holdwise.nl/en/eu-parcel-rules-2026)
- A lower fee will apply from 1 July 2028 to goods released from customs warehouses for distance sales, with the amount not yet set — [VitalLaw](https://www.vitallaw.com/news/eu-confirms-new-e-commerce-handling-fee-will-be-eur2-per-item/gdn01174716)
- A March 2026 VRT analysis still expected a Belgian handling charge of €2–3 from November — [VRT NWS, 28 Mar 2026](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/03/28/levert-de-europese-pakjestaks-belgie-enkele-miljarden-op/)

**Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (Peppol) since 1 January 2026**
- B2B structured e-invoicing has been mandatory in Belgium since 1 January 2026, and the tolerance period has expired. Businesses under the small-business VAT franchise are also covered. Even a seller that invoices only consumers usually has to be registered on Peppol to receive e-invoices from business suppliers. B2C invoices are not covered — [Unizo: e-facturatie](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/bedrijfsvoering/digitalisering/e-facturatie-correct-werken-met-peppol-en-je); [Accountable: e-facturatie](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/e-facturatie/)

### Inferences
- **Cost example for one dropshipped €20 item (single tariff category) shipped from China to a Belgian consumer, by a seller under the franchise:** customs duty €3, then import VAT at 21% on €23 (€4.83, using VRT's stated base of customs value plus duties), plus the expected €2 handling fee from about 1 Nov 2026. That is roughly **€9.83 of extra landed cost (about 49% of the goods value)**. Under the franchise this VAT is never recoverable. Under the normal VAT regime with IOSS, the seller charges 21% to the customer, but the €3 and €2 remain a cost. This arithmetic is mine; the 21% Belgian standard rate is general knowledge and was not separately sourced in this session.
- The €3 duty and €2 fee hit the China-direct dropshipping model specifically. Multi-category orders multiply the €3. The €2 fee applies even to goods above €150 and is not refunded on returns, so every withdrawal or return under consumer law (section 4) makes it a sunk cost.
- How the Belgian franchise interacts with IOSS is unclear. IOSS requires charging destination VAT at the point of sale, while the franchise means charging no Belgian VAT. A franchise seller importing into Belgium therefore appears to have to rely on import VAT being paid at the border (by the seller as importer, or by the consumer or carrier), which is a non-deductible cost. This needs confirmation from FOD Financiën or an accountant.
- Selling via a marketplace (Amazon, bol, etc.) shifts the VAT on imported consignments of €150 or less to the platform as deemed supplier. If Directive 2025/516 removes the €150 limit, it would shift VAT on all facilitated imports.
- For a zero-capital seller, the franchise is attractive (no VAT returns, prices without VAT) only if supplies are Belgian or intra-EU distance sales stay under €10,000. Above €10,000 of EU-wide distance sales, destination VAT and OSS apply.

### Gaps
- No official FOD Financiën page was readable, so the 10% tolerance rule and pro-rata examples rest on secondary sources (Accountable, Forum for the Future).
- Whether a Belgian franchise business may register for IOSS, or must register for OSS above €10,000, was not confirmed from an official source.
- Whether the €2 handling fee was actually published in the OJ and will apply from 1 Nov 2026 (no objection from Parliament or Council) could not be confirmed as of 24 Oct 2026. The unit (per item vs per declaration line) is also unresolved.
- The application date of the removal of the €150 limit in Art. 14a under Directive 2025/516 was not verified.
- Peppol access costs for a tiny business (free vs paid access points) were not found.

## 3. Product compliance: GPSR, CE marking, market surveillance, EPR (packaging, WEEE, batteries)

### Takeaway
Since 13 December 2024, the GPSR (Reg. (EU) 2023/988) bans placing consumer products on the EU market unless an **EU-established responsible economic operator** exists. Every online listing must show the manufacturer, the EU responsible person, a product identifier with image, and warnings in the local language. A Belgian dropshipper selling goods shipped straight from a Chinese factory risks being treated as the **importer**, or as the **manufacturer** if it uses its own brand, with CE-marking verification, labelling and 10-year record duties it cannot realistically meet without supplier cooperation. Belgian EPR (Fost Plus/Valipac packaging, Recupel WEEE, Bebat batteries) applies to whoever first places packaged, electrical or battery-containing products on the Belgian market. The traditional 300 kg packaging de minimis is reportedly no longer reliable since the EU PPWR became applicable on 12 August 2026.

### Cited Findings
**GPSR general obligations**
- Under GPSR Art. 16, a product cannot be placed on the EU market without a responsible economic operator established in the EU: a manufacturer, importer, authorised representative or (under conditions) a fulfilment service provider. Non-EU manufacturers must appoint an EU authorised representative — [FiveX GPSR guide](https://fivex.com/nl/blog/gtm-gpsr-explained-chinese-sellers-manufacturers/); [EUR-Lex GPSR summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/NL/legal-content/summary/general-product-safety-regulation-2023.html)
- Obligations depend on the operator's role (manufacturer, importer, distributor). They include risk assessments and technical documentation before placing on the market, labelling and traceability, and keeping product and supply-chain records for **at least 10 years** — [CMS Belgium: the new GPSR](https://cms.law/en/bel/legal-updates/the-new-eu-general-product-safety-regulation-what-you-need-to-know)
- Distributors must check that manufacturers and importers comply, refuse non-compliant products, and report dangerous products via the Safety Business Gateway. The GPSR covers new, used, repaired and refurbished consumer products sold online. Sector-specific rules (e.g. toys, machinery) take precedence — [EUR-Lex GPSR summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/NL/legal-content/summary/general-product-safety-regulation-2023.html); [EC Access2Markets GPSR](https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/nl/news/eu-verordening-inzake-algemene-productveiligheid-gpsr-een-nieuw-tijdperk-van-consumentenbescherming)
- **GPSR Art. 19 (online listings):** each offer must show the manufacturer's name, registered trade name or trademark, and postal and electronic address. Where the manufacturer is outside the EU, it must also show the EU responsible person's name and contact details. It must also show product identification (picture, type, batch or serial number where relevant) and warnings and safety information in a language consumers in that country easily understand, which in Belgium often means several languages. A separate linked page is generally not enough — [Vimm: GPSR Article 19](https://www.vimm.be/en/blog/gpsr-article-19-product-information-webshop); [GS1 GPSR guideline](https://gs1.se/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2024/12/gpsr-guideline-en.pdf)
- Marketplaces (e.g. bol, Kaufland, Amazon, eBay) require these fields per product and remove listings without them — [Vimm](https://www.vimm.be/en/blog/gpsr-article-19-product-information-webshop); [Webinterpret](https://webinterpret.com/en?p=385049)

**Dropshipper role: importer or manufacturer**
- Dutch government guidance (business.gov.nl): if you sell a product from outside the EU without holding stock, the importer rules may still apply to you. If you put your own brand on a product or modify it, you are legally the manufacturer, and you can hold several roles at once. "If you sell through dropshipping, these rules also apply to you." You should check the EU Safety Gate for known risks — [business.gov.nl: product safety for importers](https://business.gov.nl/products-services-and-innovation/product-safety/product-safety-for-importers/)
- Dutch government: a dropshipper is responsible for, among other things, product safety and delivery. (Dutch enforcement: NVWA fines; ACM up to €900,000 per infringement. This is Dutch law, included only as an EU-law analogue.) — [Rijksoverheid: dropshipping voor ondernemers](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/bescherming-van-consumenten/vraag-en-antwoord/dropshipping-voor-ondernemers)
- **Importer duties under CE legislation:** verify that the manufacturer has done conformity assessment and CE marking (documents, test reports, labels), and put the importer's name, registered trade name or trademark, and address on the product or, if that is not possible, on the packaging or an accompanying document. Under the Toy Safety Directive, a party that has a toy made and sells it under its own name or trademark takes on manufacturer obligations — [Compliance Gate: CE marking importers](https://www.compliancegate.com/ce-marking-importers/); [Compliance Gate: labelling](https://www.compliancegate.com/eu-manufacturer-importer-labelling-requirements/); [CCPC Ireland: guidance for importing products](https://www.ccpc.ie/information-for-businesses/selling-goods-and-services/product-safety/product-safety-guidance/guidance-for-importing-products); [Your Europe: product compliance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/product-requirements/compliance/index_en.htm)
- A vendor reading holds that direct shipment from a supplier still counts as placing on the EU market where the store targets the EU (EU-accessible site, EU ads, EU shipping). This is an interpretation, not authority — [Spocket: EU product safety for dropshippers](https://www.spocket.co/es/blogs/cumplimiento-de-la-normativa-de-seguridad-de-productos-de-la-ue)

**Belgian market surveillance and enforcement (FOD Economie / SPF Economie)**
- A FOD Economie spokesperson (quoted in secondary sources): dropshipping is not illegal in itself, but some webshops use misleading practices or are set up fraudulently. A typical red flag is a webshop that does not state which company is responsible and gives no contact details — [Forum for the Future: dropshipping](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/dropshipping-riskante-online-koopjes-extra-voorzichtigheid-is-geen-luxe-/29303); [VRT NWS, 6 Jan 2025](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/01/06/dropshipping-frauduleuze-webshops/)
- In 2024 the Economic Inspection found violations at 67.3% (1,794) of 2,664 businesses checked, including missing provider-identity information on websites. In a late-2025 coordinated EU webshop sweep, 69% of the Belgian companies checked did not comply. FOD Economie has shut down fraudulent sites (e.g. 39 in one action) — [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/aandachtpunt-7-op-10-gecontroleerde-bedrijven-in-overtreding-met-economische-wetgeving/28168); [Forum for the Future: 39 sites](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/fod-economie-sluit-39-frauduleuze-websites/26083); [RetailDetail](https://www.retaildetail.be/nl/news/algemeen/bijna-alle-belgische-webshops-overtreden-de-wetgeving)

**EPR: packaging (Fost Plus / Valipac / EPRiBEL)**
- A "packaging responsible" is whoever packs household products, imports packaged products into Belgium, or puts service packaging on the market. Fost Plus covers household packaging and Valipac industrial and commercial packaging. The competent authority is EPRiBEL — [RetailDetail EU](https://www.retaildetail.eu/news/general/does-your-company-meet-its-recycling-and-information-obligation/); [Unizo: verpakking](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/bedrijfsvoering/energie-en-milieu/milieu/verpakking); [WKO: PPWR Belgien](https://www.wko.at/oe/abfall/ppwr-belgien)
- **300 kg threshold:** historically, the obligation applied above 300 kg of packaging per year. According to Fost Plus and Valipac (as reported by Austria's WKO), the 300 kg threshold no longer applies because of the PPWR, so smaller volumes are also covered. Other secondary sources say the obligation now applies "from the first gram", while another says Fost Plus's checklist still uses 300 kg only as a screening point — [WKO](https://www.wko.at/oe/abfall/ppwr-belgien); [ecopv-eu](https://www.ecopv-eu.com/en/packaging-belgium-epr-guideline-2026/); [PPWR Copilot](https://ppwrcopilot.com/ppwr-be-fost-plus-declaration-deadline-guide)
- The annual declaration is due by **28 February** for the previous year. Small producers that have already filed one detailed declaration may then just report Belgian turnover — [Certivo](https://www.certivo.com/blog-details/belgium-packaging-epr-fost-plus-valipac-green-dot-and-reporting-explained); [Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/bedrijfsvoering/energie-en-milieu/milieu/verpakking)
- **EU PPWR (Reg. (EU) 2025/40)** has applied since **12 August 2026**. Art. 45(3) requires a seller supplying packaged goods directly to end users in a member state where it is not established to appoint an authorised representative there. Registration and the authorised representative are separate duties. The Commission has proposed suspending this for EU-established producers selling cross-border until 2035 (procedure 2025/0395(COD)), but as of mid-August 2026 that was still being legislated and was not law — [Dreyfus, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.dreyfus.fr/en/2026/08/20/ppwr-since-12-august-2026-packaging-epr-compliance-has-become-a-prerequisite-for-online-sales/); [expan.do](https://www.expan.do/post/authorised-representative-epr-obligation-from-12-august-2026); [Coolset](https://www.coolset.com/academy/ppwr-authorised-representative); [Lappa: PPWR postponement](https://lappa.org/blog/ppwr/what-you-need-to-know-about-ppwr-2026-postponement/)

**EPR: WEEE (Recupel) and batteries (Bebat)**
- Since 2001, Belgium has had a take-back obligation for electrical and electronic equipment, and Recupel is the collective scheme. Webshops selling into Belgium are covered: retailers delivering an appliance to a consumer's home must, on request, take back the old one free of charge — [BeCommerce/Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/wire/becommerce-kondigt-nieuw-partnerschap-recupel); [VIL: IKEO](https://vil.be/en/?p=20247); [Recupel contribution](https://www.recupel.be/en/knowledgebase/recupel-contribution/)
- Bebat: "if your webshop sells products with batteries, you should join Bebat". A webshop that buys products abroad to sell on the Belgian market is responsible for their end-of-life collection and recycling. Battery contributions apply to built-in batteries too. A battery-powered appliance on Recupel's lists also carries a Recupel contribution — [Bebat: permits webshop](https://www.bebat.be/en/blog/permits-webshop); [Recupel](https://www.recupel.be/en/knowledgebase/recupel-contribution/)
- In September 2026, Temu joined Recupel, PV CYCLE Belgium and Bebat to help its platform sellers meet EPR. This is a platform arrangement, not a legal change — [Waste Dive press release, 15 Sept 2026](https://www.wastedive.com/press-release/20260915-temu-works-with-recupel-pv-cycle-belgium-and-bebat-to-support-sellers-with/)

### Inferences
- **This is the hardest compliance area for China-direct dropshipping.** If the Chinese supplier has no EU authorised representative, the Belgian seller is the only EU-based operator and is likely to be treated as the importer. As importer, it must verify CE conformity and technical documentation, ensure its own name and address appear on the product or packaging (hard when the factory ships directly), keep records for 10 years, and handle recalls. Selling under its own brand or white-label makes it the manufacturer. Electronics, toys, cosmetics and children's products carry the highest risk.
- The practical mitigation is to source only products whose listings already name an EU responsible person, or to use EU-warehoused suppliers. This also avoids the €3 duty and €2 fee (section 2).
- If the Belgian seller is the importer of packaged goods into Belgium, it is the packaging responsible and, depending on the product, also the WEEE or battery "producer". Membership fees could not be found, but these are real recurring costs. Selling electrical items or anything containing batteries adds Recupel and Bebat duties.
- Under PPWR Art. 45(3), a Belgian seller shipping directly to consumers in other EU countries may need an authorised representative in each country unless the proposed suspension for EU producers is adopted. This is a strong reason for a zero-budget seller to sell to Belgium only at first.
- An eenmanszaak owner is personally and fully liable for product-safety failures and recalls.

### Gaps
- No FOD Economie primary page on GPSR enforcement in Belgium (designated authority, Belgian penalty levels for GPSR breaches) was retrievable.
- No authoritative EU or Belgian source explicitly classifies a dropshipper under GPSR (importer vs distributor vs neither) when goods ship directly from a non-EU factory. Only Dutch government guidance and vendor interpretations were found.
- No 2026 Fost Plus, Valipac, Recupel or Bebat membership or flat-fee tariffs for micro-sellers were found.
- The status of the Commission's PPWR authorised-representative suspension for EU producers after August 2026 is unknown.
- Costs of commercial "EU responsible person" services were not researched.

## 4. Consumer law: withdrawal, returns, warranty, mandatory website information, withdrawal button, penalties

### Takeaway
Belgian and EU law gives consumers a **14-day withdrawal right**, which is **extended by 12 months** if they were not properly informed. The trader must refund everything, including standard delivery costs. A **2-year legal guarantee** applies to new goods. Belgium transposed the **online withdrawal-button** Directive (EU) 2023/2673 late, by the law of 19 July 2026 (art. VI.61/2 WER), **in force 14 August 2026**. Webshops must show identity, KBO number, VAT number, address, phone and email on every page, and must comply with GDPR and cookie consent. FOD Economie finds non-compliance at about 2/3 of businesses it checks and can fine, refer to prosecutors or block sites.

### Cited Findings
- **Withdrawal period:** 14 calendar days from the day after the consumer physically receives the goods. If the trader did not correctly inform the consumer, the period is extended by 12 months — [Test-Aankoop: terugsturen online aankoop](https://www.test-aankoop.be/familie-prive/webshops/nieuws/terugsturen-van-onlineaankoop)
- **Refund:** all payments, including delivery costs, except the extra cost of a delivery method more expensive than the cheapest standard option. The refund must use the same payment method at no charge. (This comes from a company's own model document.) — [Telenet withdrawal document](https://www2.telenet.be/content/dam/www-telenet-be/klantenservice/downloads/herroeping-bij-overeenkomsten-op-afstandsept2020.pdf); [Captain Legal model letter](https://www.captain.legal/nl/dagelijkse-zaken/herroepingsbrief-online-aankoop-model-in-word-en-pdf/)
- Belgian consumer law applies to foreign-looking shops that target Belgian consumers, e.g. a Dutch-language site with prices in euro, even if goods ship from abroad (FOD Economie material) — [Forum for the Future: herroepingsrecht](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/herroepingsrecht-lees-dus-zeker-alles-wat-je-hierover-moet-weten-in-10-vragen-en-antwoorden./31247)
- **Withdrawal button (Directive (EU) 2023/2673):** the transposition deadline was 19 Dec 2025, with application from 19 June 2026. Belgium transposed it late by the law of **19 July 2026** (Moniteur belge 4 Aug 2026), **in force 14 August 2026**, as new art. **VI.61/2 WER**. The button must be clearly labelled, e.g. "rétractez-vous du contrat ici" or equivalent — [droitbelge.be](https://droitbelge.be/news_detail.asp?id=1199); [CMS Belgium: consumer law reform](https://cms.law/en/bel/legal-updates/belgian-consumer-law-reform-new-rules-on-online-sales-subscription-renewals-sustainability-claims-and-consumer-complaints); [Forum for the Future FR](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/fr/article/droit-de-retraction-et-commerce-en-ligne-comment-le-bouton-de-retraction-simplifie-tout/31812). **Conflict:** Altius states Belgian traders had to implement it by 19 June 2026, and a data-protection authority opinion on the draft referred to art. VI.61/1 — [Altius](https://www.altius.com/en/news/auto-renewing-a-consumer-contract-from-1-may-2027-prior-notice-is-required/); [APD/GBA decision](https://www.autoriteprotectiondonnees.be/index.php/publications/decision-2025.pdf)
- The button applies only to online contracts with a statutory withdrawal right, has no phase-in, must stay accessible for the whole withdrawal period, and requires a confirmation of receipt on a durable medium (German implementation example) — [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/5/eu-consumer-law-new-withdrawal-button-requirements-for-online-contracts); [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/blog/online-withdrawal-function-eu-directive-2023-2673/); [Andersen Belgium](https://be-api.be.andersen.com/the-eus-new-mandatory-withdrawal-button-key-compliance-steps-for-online-traders-before-june-2026/)
- **Auto-renewal notices** (Act of 20 April 2026, art. VI.91, from 1 May 2027) cover B2C service contracts only; goods-only sales fall outside — [Altius](https://www.altius.com/en/news/auto-renewing-a-consumer-contract-from-1-may-2027-prior-notice-is-required/)
- **Legal guarantee:** 2 years for new consumer goods bought from a professional seller. The consumer first chooses repair or replacement, then a price reduction or refund — [VRT NWS, 29 May 2026](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/05/29/my-jewellery-nederlandse-webwinkels-garantie/); [Unizo: garantie](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/bedrijfsvoering/marketing-en-verkoop/garantie-0)
- The 2022 reform (law of 20 March 2022, transposing Directive 2019/771) applies to sales concluded from **1 June 2022** — [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/product-defect-heb-ik-recht-op-de-wettelijke-garantie/17427); [Kamer doc 55K3492](https://www.dekamer.be/FLWB/PDF/55/3492/55K3492001.pdf). Unizo states that in the first 6 months the defect is presumed to have existed at delivery, after which the consumer may have to prove it — [Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/advies/geldt-de-wettelijke-garantie-ook-tussen-ondernemingen) (see Gaps: possibly outdated)
- **Warranty changes:** a February 2025 ministerial proposal would pause the 2-year period during repair and push for an EU-level extension from 2 to 3 years. Neither was confirmed in force — [VRT NWS, 24 Feb 2025](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/02/24/wettelijke-garantie-van-2-naar-3-jaar-in-europa-voorstel-ministe/). A new Civil Code Book 7 adds a 10-year protection from delivery, applying only from **1 September 2027** — [Test-Aankoop: 10 jaar garantie](https://www.test-aankoop.be/familie-prive/consumentenrechten/nieuws/10-jaar-garantie-belgie). From 2026, electrical appliances get a repairability score of 1–10 — [KBC](https://www.kbc.be/particulieren/nl/duurzaamheid/artikels/het-recht-op-reparatie-wat-houdt-dat-in-voor-jou.html)
- **Mandatory website information:** company identity, enterprise (KBO) number, VAT number if applicable, trade name, geographic address, phone and email must be easily accessible on all pages, plus the supervisory authority if the activity is licensed — [Securex: e-shop in 7 stappen](https://www.securex.be/nl/blog/ondernemers/uw-e-shop-wettelijk-in-orde-in-7-stappen); [Legalnews](https://legalnews.be/handel-consument/webshops-onder-de-loep-bent-u-volledig-compliant-van-havermaet/)
- Prices shown to consumers must include VAT. The ordering process and complaint procedure must be clear, and the 14-day withdrawal right with model form must be offered — [Sendcloud BE](https://www.sendcloud.com/be/veelgestelde-vragen-wettelijke-verplichtingen-voor-webshops); [SafeShops checklist](https://becom.digital/app/uploads/2022/09/VERKORT-CHECKLIST-VOOR-LABELAANVRAAG-SAFESHOPS.pdf)
- **GDPR and cookies:** visitors must be clearly informed and must consent to non-essential cookies, with a banner offering real choices. The Belgian DPA (GBA/APD) imposed a €15,000 fine in a cookie case — [Computable](https://www.computable.be/?p=10394); [Legalnews](https://legalnews.be/handel-consument/webshops-onder-de-loep-bent-u-volledig-compliant-van-havermaet/)
- **Penalties and enforcement:** Book XV WER groups infringements into six sanction levels — [news.belgium.be (2013)](https://news.belgium.be/nl/node/15389/pdf). Administrative fines run up to €10,000 for price-display breaches, and in serious cases public access to a webshop can be blocked — [Legalnews](https://legalnews.be/handel-consument/webshops-onder-de-loep-bent-u-volledig-compliant-van-havermaet/). The usual path is a warning first, then an official report (PV) to the prosecutor for repeat offenders, with a fast administrative procedure to block fraudulent sites — [FOD Economie press release (2015)](https://news.belgium.be/nl/node/18651/pdf)

### Inferences
- For China-direct dropshipping, the 14-day withdrawal right plus the full delivery-cost refund is a structural cost. Returns to China are expensive, and the €2 handling fee and €3 duty are not refunded on return. Long delivery times (weeks) also push the withdrawal window later.
- Missing withdrawal information extends exposure to 12 months and 14 days. A correct withdrawal notice, model form and (since 14 Aug 2026) a withdrawal button are cheap but essential.
- The 2-year legal guarantee rests on the Belgian seller, not the Chinese supplier, so the seller bears repair, replacement and refund costs for defective goods.
- Displaying the KBO number and identity is a legal must. It is also what FOD Economie uses to separate legitimate dropshippers from fraudulent shops.

### Gaps
- Who pays return shipping was not confirmed from a Belgian source. Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive, which I recall but did not verify in this session, the consumer bears the direct return cost if informed beforehand. Otherwise the trader bears it.
- The **presumption period** under the 2022 Belgian law was not verified. Unizo's "6 months" may predate the 2022 reform. My unverified recollection is that Belgium extended the presumption to 2 years for new goods; check art. 1649quater BW / Book 7 BW.
- The exact Belgian art. VI.61/2 wording and the mandatory label text in Dutch were not retrieved.
- The current level-2/level-3 WER fine amounts and the "opdeciemen" multiplier were not found.
- Belgian transposition status of the Right-to-Repair Directive (EU) 2024/1799 (transposition deadline 31 July 2026; 12-month guarantee extension after repair) was not confirmed. The search found no Belgian implementation.

## 5. Income tax and bookkeeping for a sole trader

### Takeaway
Sole-trader profits are taxed as professional income under progressive personal income tax at 25/40/45/50% (plus municipal surcharges). The tax-free allowance is about €10,910 for income 2025, rising under the reform law of 15 July 2026 towards €15,600 by 2029–2030. Social contributions are paid separately. Sole traders with turnover under €500,000 can keep simplified bookkeeping (financial, purchase and sales journals).

### Cited Findings
- Accountable (updated March 2026) gives brackets of 25% up to €16,720, 40% to €29,510, 45% to €51,070 and 50% above, without stating the year. The same publisher gives the 2025 brackets as 25% up to €16,320, 40% to €28,800, 45% to €49,840 and 50% above — [Accountable: progressieve belastingschijven](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/progressieve-belastingschijven/)
- The tax-free allowance is €10,910 for income 2025 and €11,180 for income year 2026 (BILLY), which is a tax-reduction mechanism, not a tax-free earnings threshold — [BILLY](https://billy.tech/nl/de-belastingschijven-en-de-belastingvrije-som-in-belgie/); [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/help-center/je-inkomstenbelasting-begrijpen-en-wat-de-belastingvrije-som-echt-betekent/)
- **Reform:** the law of 15 July 2026 reforming personal income tax was published in the Belgian Official Gazette on 29 July 2026. Some measures apply retroactively from 1 Jan or 1 Apr 2026. The tax-free allowance rises gradually from €10,910 to €15,600 (2030 per one source, 2029 per KPMG's draft summary). From income year 2027 a new **entrepreneur deduction** of 10% of profit, capped at €650 (rising to €900 in 2029), applies to self-employed natural persons. Mandatory prepayments for sole traders, and the related surcharge, disappear from income year 2026 — [Accountable: fiscale hervorming 2026](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/help-center/fiscale-hervorming-2026-wat-verandert-er-voor-zelfstandigen-in-belgie/); [Partena](https://www.partena-professional.be/nl/fiscale-hervorming-2026-4-belangrijke-wijzigingen-voor-zelfstandigen); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/be/nl/insights/my-tax-compass-de-trends-die-op-uw-fiscale-radar-moeten-staan/people-services-updates/federale-regering-keurt-wetsontwerp-hervorming-personenbelasting-goed.html); [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/lex4you/zelfstandige/nieuws/hervorming-personenbelasting,-ook-voor-mijn-eenmanszaak)
- **Bookkeeping:** sole proprietorships and certain partnerships with turnover below €500,000 excl. VAT may keep simplified bookkeeping: a financial journal (cash and bank), a purchase journal and a sales journal. Above that, double-entry bookkeeping is required. Insurance, credit and investment activities are excluded — [VLAIO: Voer een heldere boekhouding](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/verplichtingen-na-de-start/voer-een-heldere-boekhouding); [Unizo](https://www.unizo.be/ondernemersgids/financieel/fiscaliteit/zicht-op-je-boekhouding); [Xerius](https://www.xerius.be/nl-be/zelfstandig-worden/je-voorbereiding/boekhoudkundige-verplichtingen)
- Supporting documents must be kept for at least 7 years — [Accountable: boekhouding eenmanszaak](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/boekhouding-eenmanszaak/)

### Inferences
- The €16,720/€29,510/€51,070 brackets are most likely the indexed brackets for income year 2026 (assessment year 2027), since the 2025 figures are €16,320/€28,800/€49,840. This is not confirmed.
- At tiny scale (a few hundred to a few thousand euros of profit), income tax may be close to zero because of the tax-free allowance, especially for a student with little other income. Social contributions and fixed costs dominate. For a bijberoep earner, the profit stacks on top of salary at the marginal rate.
- An accountant is not legally required. A tiny eenmanszaak can keep the three journals itself, but it still needs Peppol reception (section 2).

### Gaps
- The official FOD Financiën 2026 brackets and tax-free allowance could not be read directly (€10,910 vs €11,180 for income 2026 is unresolved, and the reform may have changed the 2026 amount).
- Municipal surcharge rates were not researched.
- The 7-year vs 10-year retention period for tax documents was not resolved (only a secondary source for 7 years was found).

## 6. Starter support and status options for someone with €0 (student, unemployed, regional aid)

### Takeaway
There is no general Belgian grant for starting a dropshipping side business. The realistic zero-capital supports are: free regional first-line advice (hub.brussels "hub.info" on 1819; 1890 in Wallonia; VLAIO in Flanders); **microStart** microloans of **€500–€25,000** for people without bank access; Wallonia's **Airbag** grant of **up to €12,500** over 2 years, for main-occupation starters only; the primostarter contribution reduction; and, for the unemployed, the RVA/ONEM **"springplank naar zelfstandige"**, which allows a side activity while keeping benefits for up to 12 months. Unemployment benefits themselves are being limited in time (2 years) from 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **microStart:** loans of €500 to €25,000 for self-employed people and micro-enterprises without access to bank credit, for investment or working capital, over a maximum of 5 years. VLAIO's page (updated 12 Oct 2026) gives rates from 9%, while a June 2026 version said from 11.13%. The "Jump+" formula for starters is 12.13%, and "Shift" (green investments) a fixed 9%. Free individual coaching is provided by volunteers. A past bankruptcy or existing debts are not automatically disqualifying — [VLAIO: Microkredieten van microStart](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/microkredieten-van-microstart); [Vlaanderen.be](https://www.vlaanderen.be/microkredieten-van-microstart)
- **Wallonia, Airbag (Forem):** up to €12,500 over two years, paid in four instalments, for people becoming self-employed **in a main occupation** for the first or second time. You must have followed IFAPME training or be supported by a SAACE, and be domiciled or have your seat in Wallonia. The application must be filed before joining a social insurance fund or at the latest within one month after. An alternative route requires at least 3 years as a side-occupation self-employed person. Reforms of Airbag and "Starter Wallonia" have reportedly been approved by the Walloon Parliament, with details unconfirmed — [Le Forem: Airbag](https://www.leforem.be/entreprises/aides-financieres-creation-activite-independant-airbag.html); [MyFid](https://www.myfid.be/ressources/creation-entreprise/aides-primes-subsides-creation-entreprise-wallonie/); [LinkedIn post (low reliability)](https://be.linkedin.com/in/lionelbonjean)
- **Brussels, hub.brussels "hub.info":** free first-line information for starting entrepreneurs in FR/NL/EN, on freephone 1819, at info@hub.brussels, or walk-in at Chaussée de Charleroi 110, Saint-Gilles. It also offers free customised coaching and refers to partners. **1890** is Wallonia's equivalent line — [hub.info](https://info.hub.brussels/en/node/92); [be.brussels: hub.brussels](https://be.brussels/en/about-region/structure-and-organisations/overview-administrations-and-institutions-region/hubbrussels); [hub.brussels](https://hub.brussels/?p=436381)
- **Flanders, VLAIO:** start guidance, a student-entrepreneur status page and a subsidy database, including the primostarter reduction ("Starterskorting") — [VLAIO Starterskorting](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/verlaging-sociale-bijdragen-voor-primostarters-starterskorting); [VLAIO: starten vanuit werkloosheid](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/starten-vanuit-werkloosheid-steunmogelijkheden)
- **Unemployed, "Springplank naar zelfstandige" (RVA/ONEM, infoblad T158):** you keep the right to unemployment benefits while running a side activity as self-employed for at most 12 months. You must declare the activity at the benefit application or before starting (form C1C), and you may not have run it as a main-occupation self-employed person in the past 6 years. Benefits can be reduced according to side income, and are lost for days you work in the activity between 7:00 and 18:00 on weekdays — [VLAIO: starten vanuit werkloosheid](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/starten-vanuit-werkloosheid-steunmogelijkheden); [ACV United Freelancers](https://www.hetacv.be/united-freelancers/werken-als-freelancer/zelfstandig-en-werkloos); [VLAIO: bijberoep en werkloos](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/werkloosheid-en-ondernemen/je-hebt-een-zelfstandig-bijberoep-en-wordt)
- After starting as a main-occupation self-employed person, people on unemployment or integration benefits can keep their right to benefits for 15 years (a fallback if the business fails) — [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/subsidies-financiering/subsidiedatabank/starten-vanuit-werkloosheid-steunmogelijkheden)
- **Time limit on unemployment benefits:** from 2026, the maximum duration is phased down to 2 years — [RIZIV/INAMI](https://www.inami.fgov.be/nl/thema-s/arbeidsongeschiktheid/werknemers-en-werklozen/beperking-van-werkloosheid-in-de-tijd-impact-op-uw-recht-op-arbeidsongeschiktheids-of-moederschapsuitkeringen); [VRT NWS, 7 June 2025](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/06/07/beperking-werkloosheidsuitkering-in-tijd-krijgt-groen-licht-in-k/)
- **Students:** the student-entrepreneur status (section 1) allows starting a business while studying, with €0 contributions below €8,687.04 net taxable income, on request — [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/student-zelfstandige/veelgestelde-vragen/veelgestelde-vragen-na-de-opstart-van-je-student-onderneming)

### Inferences
- For a €0-capital dropshipper the most useful supports are free advice (VLAIO, hub.info 1819, 1890) and status choice (student or springplank). microStart could finance working capital, but at 9–12% interest a loan is risky for an unproven dropshipping model. Airbag requires main-occupation status and IFAPME or SAACE support, so it does not fit a side activity.
- An unemployed person can test the business for up to 12 months via springplank without losing benefits. With the 2-year limit on benefits being phased in, the timing of that test window matters.
- The **overbruggingsrecht / droit passerelle** is a replacement income for self-employed people who *stop* (bankruptcy, forced cessation, economic difficulty). It is not starting aid (general knowledge, not verified in this session; see Gaps).

### Gaps
- Overbruggingsrecht 2026 amounts and conditions were not researched or sourced.
- Unizo and UCM starter-service offerings and costs (free vs member-only) were not found.
- How the 2-year unemployment-benefit limit interacts with the 12-month springplank was not confirmed.
- The content of the 2026 Airbag and Starter Wallonia reform was not found.
- No Flemish or Brussels starter grant applicable to e-commerce side activities was found (e.g. the KMO-portefeuille was not researched).

## 7. Total minimum unavoidable cost per year to legally run a tiny Belgian online store

### Takeaway
The legal floor is about **€111.50 in year 1** (KBO registration only) for a student-entrepreneur or a bijberoep earner with very low profit who uses the VAT franchise and files the VAT activation themselves. A main-occupation primostarter pays about **€2,007–€2,029 in year 1**, and a regular main-occupation starter about **€3,780–€3,820 per year**, before any product-specific costs. On top of this come unpriced but legally relevant items: EPR memberships, an EU responsible person under GPSR, and Peppol reception. China-direct dropshipping also carries per-order duties and fees (€3 per tariff category plus €2 per item from about Nov 2026, plus non-deductible import VAT).

### Cited Findings (inputs used)
- KBO registration €111.50 (one-off; each later change also €111.50) — [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/opstart-van-je-onderneming/een-ondernemingsnummer-aanvragen)
- VAT number activation via a loket €60 excl. VAT (Securex), or self-filed online via MyMinfin e604. No official fee for self-filing was found — [Securex](https://www.securex.be/nl/starten-als-zelfstandige/een-eigen-zaak-starten/ondernemingsnummer-aanvragen); [Forum for the Future](https://blog.forumforthefuture.be/nl/article/aanvang-wijziging-en-einde-activiteit-de-toepassing-e604-is-nu-ook-rechtstreeks-toegankelijk-via-myminfin/25946)
- Social contributions in 2026: main occupation €890.42 per quarter; primostarter €459.82 per quarter; bijberoep €98.51 per quarter minimum, €0 below €1,922.16; student €0 below €8,687.04 — [RSVZ 2026 table](https://socialsecurity.belgium.be/sites/default/files/content/docs/nl/sociaal-beleid-vorm-geven/zelfstandigen/bijdragennota_2026_tabel_nl.pdf); [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/sociale-bijdragen); [VLAIO](https://www.vlaio.be/nl/begeleiding-advies/start/student-zelfstandige/veelgestelde-vragen/veelgestelde-vragen-na-de-opstart-van-je-student-onderneming)
- Fund management fees 3.05–4.25% of contributions — [BILLY](https://billy.tech/nl/gids/onderneming-starten/stappen/administratieve-formaliteiten/sociaal-verzekeringsfonds-kiezen/)
- €3 duty per tariff category (consignments of €150 or less, since 1 July 2026); €2 per item handling fee (expected from 1 Nov 2026) — [Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/11/council-gives-final-green-light-to-new-customs-duty-rules-for-small-parcels/); [VitalLaw](https://www.vitallaw.com/news/eu-confirms-new-e-commerce-handling-fee-will-be-eur2-per-item/gdn01174716)

### Inferences (my calculations)
| Scenario | Year-1 fixed legal costs | Following years (fixed) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Student-zelfstandige, net income < €8,687.04, VAT franchise, self-filed VAT activation | **€111.50** | **€0** (if the contribution exemption is requested each year) | VLAIO advises paying €98.51/q if unsure: €394.04/yr + about 3–4% fees (≈ €406–411) |
| B. Bijberoep (employee ≥ 50%), profit < €1,922.16 | **€111.50** (+ any provisional €98.51/q billed, ≈ €406–411/yr, adjusted on regularisation) | ≈ €0 final contributions | Contributions above €1,922.16 at 20.5% |
| C. Main-occupation primostarter | €111.50 + €459.82 × 4 = €1,839.28 + fees (€56–78) ≈ **€2,007–€2,029** | Moves to regular minimum or income-based | Reduced base €8,972.07 |
| D. Main occupation, regular minimum | €111.50 + €890.42 × 4 = €3,561.68 + fees (€109–151) ≈ **€3,782–€3,824** | ≈ €3,671–€3,713/yr | Minimum base €17,374.08 |
| Optional: VAT activation via loket | + €60 excl. VAT (€72.60 incl. 21%) | — | Avoidable by self-filing on MyMinfin |

- **Variable per-order legal costs for China-direct goods** (example of a €20 single-category item to a Belgian consumer under the franchise): €3 duty + €4.83 import VAT + €2 handling fee (from about Nov 2026) ≈ **€9.83 per item**, none recoverable. Returns do not refund the €2 fee.
- **Unpriced but potentially mandatory items:** Fost Plus or Valipac membership if the seller is the Belgian importer of packaged goods (300 kg de minimis possibly gone); Recupel and Bebat for electronics or batteries; an EU responsible person if the Chinese manufacturer has none (GPSR); Peppol reception capability; PPWR authorised representatives for direct sales into other EU countries. An accountant, website, domain, cookie tool and T&C drafting are practically necessary but not legally priced.
- **Bottom line for the report:** the absolute legal minimum to start is about €111.50 plus €0 contributions, achievable only as an eligible student-entrepreneur or as a bijberoep earner with very low profit. Any non-student without a qualifying ≥ 50% job is pushed into main-occupation status and faces about €1,840–€3,560/year in minimum social contributions plus fees, regardless of sales. The China-direct model adds per-order costs and compliance duties (GPSR importer role, EPR, 2-year guarantee, withdrawal refunds) that are hard to meet at €0. An EU-warehouse supplier that already provides an EU responsible person is the lower-risk variant.

### Gaps
- No 2026 tariffs were found for Fost Plus/Valipac/Recupel/Bebat micro-members, GPSR EU-representative services, or Peppol access points, so the "true" annual minimum for China-direct dropshipping cannot be fully priced.
- Whether a social insurance fund charges any fixed annual fee when contributions are €0 (student exemption, low-income bijberoep) was not found.
